A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before you
take the first step.

How does the quote process work?+

Build your design, add your contact details, and open the prepared request in WhatsApp or your email app. Send the message to Steve Craft. We’ll discuss materials, fit, final pricing and production timing before you place an order.

Will my footwear look exactly like the preview?+

The studio is an illustrative design guide. Natural materials, screens and construction can affect the final appearance. Steve Craft will confirm the available materials and design details with you before your order.

I’m unsure about my size. Can you help?+

Yes. Choose your usual EU size as a starting point and mention any fit concerns in your request. You can also include your foot length and width. We’ll confirm sizing with you before production.

Can I ask for something outside the studio options?+

Absolutely. Add your idea in the notes when requesting a quote, or message Steve Craft directly. Availability and any additional work will be discussed as part of your quote.

How long will my pair take, and what will it cost?+

Each design is quoted individually. Steve Craft will provide the price, production timing and delivery arrangements after reviewing your request. No payment is collected on this website.
